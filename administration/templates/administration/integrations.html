Prometheus service discovery

Token and fetch record{% icon "chevron-right" 13 %}

Paste this once into prometheus.yml. Every target you onboard is then scraped on Prometheus’s next discovery cycle, with no further config edit. The bearer token is generated (and rotated) on the service discovery page, where it is shown once.

scrape_configs:
  - job_name: linkedeye-sd
    http_sd_configs:
      - url: {{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}{% url 'onboarding:sd_targets' %}
        refresh_interval: 60s
        authorization:
          type: Bearer
          credentials: <token from the service discovery page>
